BANKWELL FINANCIAL GROUP REPORTS RECORD EARNINGS FOR THE THIRD QUARTER, DECLARES FOURTH QUARTER DIVIDEND
New Canaan, CT – October 29, 2026 – Bankwell Financial Group, Inc. (NASDAQ: BWFG) reported GAAP net income of $12.7 million, or $1.58 per share for the third quarter of 2026, versus $12.4 million, or $1.52 per share, for the second quarter of 2026. The Company's Board of Directors declared a $0.20 per share cash dividend, payable November 20, 2026 to shareholders of record on November 9, 2026.
The Company’s reported results include $3.0 million of non-interest expense attributed to core conversion costs incurred during the third quarter of 2026. For the third quarter of 2026, the Company reported GAAP net income of $12.7 million and operating net income(1) of $15.0 million. Operating results are non-GAAP financial measures that exclude certain nonrecurring or transaction-related expenses. Management believes these measures provide additional insight into underlying operating performance; however, they should be considered in addition to, and not as a substitute for, results prepared in accordance with GAAP.

Discussion of Outlook; Bankwell Financial Group Chief Executive Officer, Christopher R. Gruseke:
"We delivered record earnings for the third quarter, with GAAP net income of $12.7 million, or $1.58 per share, despite absorbing $3.0 million of costs related to our core conversion. Excluding these costs, operating net income was $15.0 million, or $1.86 per share, and our operating efficiency ratio was 47.5%. These results reflect continued improvement across the Company, as Net Interest Margin expanded to 3.71%, nonperforming assets declined to 0.20% of total assets, and our SBA business continued to make a meaningful contribution to non-interest income.
Our third-quarter results demonstrate the benefit of the investments and strategic initiatives we have pursued over the past several years. We continue to grow the balance sheet, diversify revenue, strengthen asset quality and invest in the people and technology necessary to support the Company’s future growth. The core conversion is an important part of that investment and will provide enhanced capabilities and greater scalability to support our strategic priorities and future growth.
Given our year-to-date performance, we are updating our full-year 2026 guidance. We now expect net interest income of $119 to $121 million and non-interest income of $14 to $15 million, reflecting continued strength in our SBA platform. We maintain our outlook for loan growth of 5% to 7% and non-interest expense of $65 to $67 million, excluding conversion costs.
Finally, I want to recognize the tremendous efforts of our team. The results we are reporting are the product of a great deal of hard work, an unwavering commitment to our clients, and disciplined execution of the strategy we have put in place. I’m proud of what our people have accomplished together and excited about the opportunities ahead."

Key Points for Third Quarter and Bankwell’s Outlook
Asset Quality Strengthens; Capital Remains Strong
•Nonperforming loans decreased $8.7 million to $7.1 million in the third-quarter of 2026, primarily reflecting the note sale of a suburban retail CRE loan in Westchester County, NY, that had been modified during the COVID-19 pandemic.
•Nonperforming assets declined to 0.20% of total assets at September 30, 2026, compared with 0.46% at June 30, 2026.
•Net recoveries totaled $1.4 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. The ACL-Loans provided 430.65% coverage of total nonperforming loans at September 30, 2026 compared to 193.14% at June 30, 2026.
•Tangible common equity to tangible assets increased to 9.33%, and fully diluted tangible book value per common share increased to $41.59.
Balance Sheet Growth and Continued Net Interest Margin Expansion
•Gross loans increased $22.9 million during the quarter and $142.6 million, or 5.0% year-to-date.
•Net interest margin expanded 13 basis points to 3.71% for the third quarter of 2026, compared to 3.58% for the second quarter of 2026, reflecting higher earning-asset yields and flat funding costs.
•Net interest income increased to $31.4 million, compared with $29.5 million in the linked quarter.
•Earning asset yields expanded 8 basis points to 6.34% for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to the linked quarter.
Strong SBA Performance Drives Noninterest Income Growth
•SBA loan sale gains contributed $2.8 million of the Company's $4.0 million in total noninterest income for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, bringing 2026 year-to-date gains to $7.6 million, compared to $2.9 million 2025 year-to-date gains.
•Noninterest income as a percentage of revenue for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 was 11.20%, compared to 8.76% for the quarter ended September 30, 2025.
•The SBA Lending division delivered $30.1 million in originations in the third quarter of 2026, bringing the total year-to-date originations to $90.2 million.
Investment in Technology and Operating Infrastructure
•The Company is undertaking a core conversion initiative as part of its broader investment in technology and operating infrastructure to expand capabilities, improve efficiency and scalability, and support future growth.
•Third-quarter results included $3.0 million of core conversion costs, primarily consisting of termination fees.
•Excluding these costs, operating net income was $15.0 million(1), operating diluted EPS was $1.86(1) and the operating efficiency ratio was 47.5%(1), demonstrating the strength of the Company's underlying operating performance.
•The Company expects to incur additional core conversion-related costs in future quarters as implementation activities progress; however, third-quarter costs were elevated by termination-related costs that are not expected to recur in future periods.

About Bankwell Financial Group
Bankwell Financial Group, Inc. is the holding company for Bankwell Bank ("Bankwell"), a full-service commercial bank headquartered in New Canaan, CT. Bankwell provides businesses and professionals with a range of commercial financing solutions, including working capital lines of credit, SBA loans, acquisition financing, and commercial mortgages, along with treasury management and deposit services. Bankwell emphasizes accessibility, expertise, and responsiveness through experienced local banking teams serving its markets.
