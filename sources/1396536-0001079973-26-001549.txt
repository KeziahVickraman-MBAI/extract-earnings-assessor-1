Duos Technologies Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
Q3 2026 Revenue Increased 103%, Driven by Technology Solutions Demand and Infrastructure Development Activity
Signed 10-Year, 20 MW, $400 Million Colocation Agreement with a Publicly Traded Cloud Services Provider
Reaffirms 2026 Outlook of Over $50 Million in Revenue and Adjusted EBITDA Positivity
JACKSONVILLE, FL / Globe Newswire / November 16, 2026 - Duos Technologies Group, Inc. (“Duos” or the “Company”) (Nasdaq: DUOT), a provider of adaptive, modular architecture compute center colocation and infrastructure solutions, reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026 (“Q3 2026”).
Third Quarter 2026 and Recent Operational Highlights
• | Completed the strategic repositioning of the Company into a colocation and infrastructure business for compute centers. Using its modular data center architecture, the Company is focused on distributed sites of 5 to 20 MW.
• | Secured a 10-year, 20 MW colocation agreement from a mature, publicly traded Cloud Service Provider (“CSP”). The agreement is valued at more than $400 million in total contract value, and deployment is expected to commence in Q2 2027.
• | Completed the sale of the Company’s GPU-as-a-Service Entity to Axe Compute. The sale eliminated approximately $87.8 million of prospective equipment debt and frees capital to support future modular data center development.
• | Advanced development plans to secure approximately 100 MW of additional powered, data center-zoned land across multiple U.S. markets. Of that total, approximately 25 MW is currently under contract across multiple sites, and the Company is in active negotiations on an additional 75 MW.
• | Strengthened the executive leadership team with appointments of Dipan Patel as Chief Operating Officer, Christopher DeAlmeida as Chief Financial Officer, and Matthew Polimeni as Chief Legal Officer.
Third Quarter 2026 Consolidated Financial Results
Revenues for Q3 2026 increased 103% to $10.50 million compared to $5.18 million for the third quarter of 2025 (“Q3 2025”). Consolidated revenue was comprised of approximately $6.84 million of Technology Solutions, $3.61 million of Services and Consulting (including related-party activity), and $0.05 million of Hosting and Colocation revenue. The increase was primarily driven by Technology Solutions growth and revenue associated with a data center site-development advisory agreement, partially offset by the wind-down of legacy asset-management activity.
The Technology Solutions business unit provides manufacturer-agnostic infrastructure sourcing, integration, and value-added supply chain services for data center, AI, and enterprise deployments. It also gives the Company procurement scale and supply chain access that supports its own modular data center buildout. Looking ahead, the Company expects revenue to shift toward long-term, recurring colocation and infrastructure contracts as it builds out distributed compute sites in the 5 to 20 MW range using its modular architecture. That growth is expected to come from three sources: capacity coming online at existing sites, the 20 MW CSP deployment expected to commence in Q2 2027, and development of the approximately 100 MW of powered land the Company is securing across multiple U.S. markets. Technology Solutions is expected to keep growing alongside demand in the broader data center market.
Cost of revenues for Q3 2026 increased 76% to $6.14 million compared to $3.48 million for Q3 2025. The increase was primarily driven by higher equipment and integration costs associated with Technology Solutions deliveries and costs associated with the Company’s infrastructure-related services.
Gross profit for Q3 2026 increased 156% to $4.36 million compared to $1.70 million for Q3 2025. The increase reflected greater Technology Solutions activity and revenue associated with the Company’s infrastructure-development arrangements. As a percentage of revenue, gross margin improved to 41.6% from 32.9% in Q3 2025.
Operating expenses for Q3 2026 increased 140% to $6.22 million compared to $2.59 million for Q3 2025. The increase was primarily driven by higher stock-based compensation, sales and marketing expenses, and professional fees associated with the Company’s strategic transactions and business transformation. Noncash stock-based compensation totaled approximately $2.30 million in the quarter.
Loss from operations for Q3 2026 totaled $1.86 million compared to $0.89 million for Q3 2025. Gross profit increased approximately $2.66 million, but this was more than offset by a $3.63 million increase in operating expenses, primarily due to higher noncash stock-based compensation.
Loss before income taxes from continuing operations for Q3 2026 totaled $0.49 million compared with a loss of $0.81 million for Q3 2025. The narrower pretax loss reflected approximately $1.37 million of net other income, including a $4.70 million gain on the GPU-as-a-Service deconsolidation and $0.74 million of interest income, partly offset by a $2.82 million loss on debt extinguishment and $1.56 million of interest expense. Including discontinued operations, the Company reported a net income of $864 thousand versus a net loss of $1.04 million in Q3 2025. Basic and diluted net income per common share were $0.02 for Q3 2026 versus a net loss of $0.06 per common share for Q3 2025.
Cash and cash equivalents as of September 30, 2026, totaled $44.81 million compared to $15.47 million as of December 31, 2025. In addition, the September 30, 2026, balance sheet included approximately $9.76 million in net accounts receivable, $3.46 million in contract assets, $10.01 million in a transaction holdback receivable, and $42.90 million in deferred transaction consideration receivable.
Nine-Month 2026 Consolidated Financial Results
Revenues for the first nine months of 2026 increased 36% to $18.82 million compared to $13.87 million in the same period of 2025. Revenue comprised approximately $10.64 million of Technology Solutions, $8.07 million of Services and Consulting (including related-party activity), and $0.11 million of Hosting and Colocation. Growth reflected Technology Solutions activity and data center site-development advisory revenue, partly offset by reduced revenue from the legacy asset-management arrangement.
Cost of revenues for the first nine months of 2026 increased 9% to $9.96 million compared to $9.13 million in the same period of 2025. Increased Technology Solutions fulfillment costs and infrastructure-services activity were partly offset by lower costs following the wind-down of the legacy asset-management arrangement.
Gross profit for the first nine months of 2026 increased 87% to $8.86 million compared to $4.74 million in the prior-year period. Revenue grew 36% while cost of revenues increased 9%. As a percentage of revenue, gross margin improved to 47.1% from 34.2%.
Operating expenses for the first nine months of 2026 increased 80% to $13.85 million compared to $7.70 million in the prior-year period. The increase was primarily driven by noncash stock-based compensation, expanded sales and marketing activity, and professional fees related to strategic transactions and the Company’s infrastructure development.
Loss from operations for the first nine months of 2026 totaled $4.98 million compared to $2.96 million for the same period in 2025. A $6.15 million increase in operating expenses more than offset a $4.13 million increase in gross profit.
Income before income taxes from continuing operations for the first nine months of 2026 totaled $50.11 million compared to a pretax loss of $3.25 million in the prior-year period. The improvement was primarily driven by the gain associated with the New APR Energy investment sale and other nonoperating gains, partly offset by the operating loss. After income taxes and discontinued operations, consolidated net income was $45.22 million compared with a net loss of $6.64 million in 2025. Basic and diluted net income per common share were $1.51 and $1.29, respectively, for the first nine months of 2026, compared with a net loss of $0.49 per share (basic and diluted) in 2025.
Financial Outlook
The Company enters the fourth quarter with its strategic repositioning into a compute center colocation and infrastructure provider complete. Its operating model is now built around long-term, recurring contracts. Fourth-quarter results are expected to reflect a full quarter of contribution from the hosting capacity that came online at the end of the third quarter, along with continued Technology Solutions deliveries under existing customer orders. Based on contracts already performing and orders scheduled for delivery in the fourth quarter, the Company continues to expect that its total revenue for 2026 will exceed $50 million.
Looking ahead, the Company’s near-term priorities are:
• | Finalizing site agreements on approximately 100 MW of powered, data center-zoned land;
• | Preparing for commencement of the 20 MW CSP deployment in Q2 2027;
• | Securing non-dilutive financing to fund its modular buildout.
The Company believes these steps position it to scale recurring colocation revenue across distributed 5 to 20 MW sites in a market where demand for available, powered capacity continues to exceed supply.
Adjusted EBITDA for the third quarter of 2026 was $493 thousand. The Company did not report adjusted EBITDA in the prior-year period. The Company expects profitability to continue to improve as revenue ramps over the coming quarters and anticipates achieving positive adjusted EBITDA for the full year 2026.
Management Commentary
“In the third quarter we fully completed our evolution into a pure-play colocation company, and our focus is now strictly on acquiring, developing, and operating additional 5 to 20 MW modular colocation sites across the U.S.,” said Duos CEO Doug Recker. “We have multiple long-term contracts with high-quality customers, an operating flagship campus with additional sites identified, and a strong backlog across multiple business lines providing a visible path to scale. The job now is to advance the platform. Our recent $400 million, 20 MW colocation agreement with a major CSP, combined with our plans to finalize up to 100 MW of additional powered, data center-zoned land, underscore the increasing demand we are seeing from customers seeking scalable, power-dense infrastructure that can be deployed quickly and efficiently. As a result of our progress to date, we remain on track to meet our goals for the year, including revenue north of $50 million and positive adjusted EBITDA.”

About Duos Technologies Group, Inc.
Duos Technologies Group, Inc. (Nasdaq: DUOT), headquartered in Jacksonville, Florida, provides and manages modular architecture colocation data centers and infrastructure solutions. Through its Duos Edge AI brand, the Company delivers high-performance computing infrastructure built on its proprietary, scalable Duos Modular Architecture, designed to support the high-power environments required for AI and enterprise computing. Separately, Duos Technology Solutions provides manufacturer-agnostic sourcing and fulfillment services that enable efficient deployment of data centers and IT environments. Together, these platforms position the Company to address growing demand for distributed digital infrastructure in underserved Tier 3 and Tier 4 markets. For more information, visit www.duostech.com and www.duosedge.ai.
