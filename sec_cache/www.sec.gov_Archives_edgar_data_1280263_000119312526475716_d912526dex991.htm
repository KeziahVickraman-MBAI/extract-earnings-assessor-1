Ambarella, Inc. Announces Third Quarter Fiscal Year 2027 Financial Results

November 24, 2026 —Santa Clara, Calif. – Ambarella, Inc. (NASDAQ: AMBA), an edge AI semiconductor company, today announced third quarter fiscal 2027 financial results for the period ended October 31, 2026.

 

  •  

Revenue for the third quarter of fiscal 2027 was $115.2 million, up 6.2% from $108.5 million in the same period in fiscal 2026. For the nine months ended October 31, 2026, revenue was $323.7 million, up 11.7% from $289.8 million for the nine months ended October 31, 2025.

 

  •  

Gross margin under U.S. generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP) for the third quarter of fiscal 2027 was 57.8%, compared with 59.6% for the same period in fiscal 2026. For the nine months ended October 31, 2026, GAAP gross margin was 57.9%, compared with 59.5% for the nine months ended October 31, 2025.

 

  •  

GAAP net loss for the third quarter of fiscal 2027 was $12.7 million, or loss per diluted ordinary share of $0.29, compared with a GAAP net loss of $15.1 million, or loss per diluted ordinary share of $0.35, for the same period in fiscal 2026. GAAP net loss for the nine months ended October 31, 2026 was $37.5 million or loss per diluted ordinary share of $0.85. This compares with GAAP net loss of $59.4 million, or loss per diluted ordinary share of $1.40, for the nine months ended October 31, 2025.

Financial results on a non-GAAP basis for the third quarter of fiscal 2027 are as follows:

 

  •  

Gross margin on a non-GAAP basis for the third quarter of fiscal 2027 was 59.5%, compared with 60.9% for the same period in fiscal 2026. For the nine months ended October 31, 2026, non-GAAP gross margin was 59.6%, compared with 61.1% for the nine months ended October 31, 2025.

 

  •  

Non-GAAP net income for the third quarter of fiscal 2027 was $12.7 million, or earnings per diluted ordinary share of $0.28. This compares with non-GAAP net income of $11.9 million, or earnings per diluted ordinary share of $0.27, for the same period in fiscal 2026. Non-GAAP net income for the nine months ended October 31, 2026 was $26.0 million, or earnings per diluted ordinary share of $0.58. This compares with non-GAAP net income of $21.3 million, or earnings per diluted ordinary share of $0.49, for the nine months ended October 31, 2025.

Based on information available as of today, Ambarella is offering the following guidance for the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2027, ending January 31, 2027:

 

  •  

Revenue is expected to be between $70.0 million and $75.0 million.

 

  •  

Gross margin on a non-GAAP basis is expected to be between 59.0% and 60.25%.

 

  •  

Non-GAAP operating expenses are expected to be between $57.5 million and $59.5 million.

Ambarella reports gross margin, net income (loss) and earnings (losses) per share in accordance with GAAP and, additionally, on a non-GAAP basis. Non-GAAP financial information excludes the impact of stock-based compensation, nonrecurring development project termination credit and acquisition-

related costs adjusted for the associated tax impact, which includes the effect of any benefits or shortfalls recognized. A reconciliation of the GAAP to non-GAAP gross margin, net income (loss) and earnings (losses) per share for the periods presented, as well as a description of the items excluded from the non-GAAP calculations, is included in the financial statements portion of this press release.

Total cash, cash equivalents and marketable debt securities on hand at the end of the third quarter of fiscal 2027 was $282.8 million, compared with $272.3 million at the end of the prior quarter and $295.3 million at the end of the same quarter a year ago.

“We reported record edge AI revenue in our third quarter, although total revenue was at the low end of our guidance range as our consumer IoT revenue was below our expectations. The unprecedented rise in memory prices and the scarcity of memory supply we previously highlighted has intensified and is causing many customers to reduce their outlooks”, said Fermi Wang, President & CEO. “Memory related challenges do not have an impact on our very positive secular growth outlook for edge AI and our positioning and investment therein. Physical AI brings domain specific context to LLMs running in the datacenter, which differentiates and improves the overall combined edge AI and cloud AI value proposition. Our investments in new products, expanding Partner program, enhancements to our Cooper Development Platform and reach into new applications is expected to enable our fiscal 2028 to be a growth year.”

About Ambarella

With an installed base of more than 55 million AI SoC units, Ambarella’s products are utilized in a wide variety of physical edge AI applications, spanning edge endpoint and edge infrastructure use cases including physical security, vehicle safety, telematics, autonomy, portable video, aerial drones, and other emerging robotic applications. Building on this footprint, Ambarella offers a full-stack edge AI platform, from highly optimized silicon and programmable software to AI agentic frameworks that coordinate perception, decision-making and control across devices. Ambarella’s low-power systems-on-chip (SoCs) integrate proprietary and highly efficient perception and deep learning neural network AI accelerators, enabling electronic systems to become more productive with partial or complete levels of machine autonomy. For more information, please visit www.ambarella.com.
