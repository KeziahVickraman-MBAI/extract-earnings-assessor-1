THE MARZETTI COMPANY REPORTS FIRST QUARTER SALES AND EARNINGS
WESTERVILLE, Ohio, November 3 - The Marzetti Company (Nasdaq: MZTI) reported results today for the company’s fiscal first quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Summary
•Consolidated first quarter net sales declined 2.4% to $481.5 million. Excluding the prior-year quarter’s $10.7 million in non-core sales attributed to a temporary supply agreement (“TSA”) with Winland Foods, Inc., which concluded during the quarter ended March 31, 2026, consolidated net sales declined 0.3%.
•Retail segment net sales increased 2.0% to $252.7 million, which includes $17.8 million of incremental sales from Bachan’s, Inc., our newly acquired Japanese Barbecue Sauce brand known for its delicious, authentic, clean-label products.
•Foodservice segment net sales decreased 6.9% to $228.8 million on a reported basis. Excluding the non-core TSA sales of $10.7 million in the prior-year quarter, Foodservice segment net sales decreased 2.6%.
•Both Retail and Foodservice segment net sales were significantly impacted by the cyclospora outbreak.
•Consolidated gross profit declined $8.0 million to $110.8 million and reported gross profit margin decreased 110 basis points to 23.0% driven by the reduced sales and associated impact of lower production volumes on our plant operations; significantly higher diesel and freight costs; and commissioning costs for the addition of new production capacity at our sauce and dressing facility in Atlanta, Georgia.
•SG&A expenses increased $10.9 million to $69.3 million. The higher SG&A expenses were primarily driven by incremental core SG&A costs for Bachan’s in addition to $2.4 million in noncash amortization expense attributed to Bachan’s intangible assets.
•Consolidated operating income declined $17.8 million to $41.4 million, which reflects the impact of the lower gross profit and higher SG&A expenses. Prior-year operating income includes restructuring and impairment charges of $1.1 million resulting from the closure of our sauce and dressing facility in Milpitas, California. Adjusted Operating Income declined $16.6 million to $43.9 million.
•First quarter net income was $1.07 per diluted share versus $1.71 per diluted share last year. In the current-year quarter, the incremental noncash amortization expense for Bachan’s intangible assets reduced net income by $0.07 per diluted share. In the prior-year quarter, restructuring and impairment charges reduced net income by $0.03 per diluted share. Excluding these items, the resulting Adjusted Net Income Per Diluted Share (“Adjusted Diluted EPS”) was $1.14 in the current-year quarter, versus $1.74 last year, as impacted by the reduced gross profit and higher SG&A expenses.

CEO David A. Ciesinski commented, “The cyclospora outbreak had a significant impact on this quarter’s top and bottom-line results. The outbreak resulted in lower sales for both our Retail and Foodservice segments, with the impact on Retail segment net sales more notable, accounting for an estimated year-over-year net sales decline of approximately 4% due to lower demand for our Retail dressing, dip and crouton products. On a positive note, other areas of our business continued to perform well and the cyclospora impact has moderated in recent weeks.”
“The newly acquired Bachan’s brand added $17.8 million in incremental sales to our Retail segment. Retail scanner data sourced from Circana for the quarter ended September shows continued momentum for the Bachan’s brand, with sales up 10.7% as the brand continues to gain share in the barbecue sauce category. Consumer demand for our Texas Roadhouse® dinner rolls also remained strong, with sales up 21.3% per Circana. In the Foodservice segment, with the cyclospora outbreak a headwind to growth, the segment’s reported net sales declined 6.9% and Adjusted Foodservice Net Sales, which exclude the non-core TSA sales, decreased 2.6%.”
“The decrease in consolidated operating income was driven by the lower sales and associated impact of lower production volumes on our plant operations; significantly higher diesel and freight costs; and commissioning costs for the addition of new production capacity at our sauce and dressing facility in Atlanta, Georgia.”
“Looking ahead to our fiscal second quarter and the remainder of the fiscal year, we anticipate Retail segment sales will continue to benefit from incremental sales attributed to the Bachan’s acquisition in addition to contributions from new items that we recently launched or have planned for introduction later this year. In the Foodservice segment, we expect sales to remain supported by select quick-service restaurant customers in our mix of national chain restaurant accounts. We continue to anticipate a moderate level of commodity cost inflation, in addition to significantly higher diesel and freight costs, most or all of which we expect to mitigate through inflationary pricing and our cost savings programs as we remain focused on margin improvement.”
First Quarter Results
Consolidated net sales decreased 2.4% to $481.5 million versus $493.5 million last year. Excluding the prior-year quarter’s $10.7 million in non-core sales attributed to the TSA with Winland Foods, Inc., Adjusted Consolidated Net Sales declined 0.3%. Our newly acquired Bachan’s business added $17.8 million in net sales, or about 360 basis points of consolidated net sales growth. Retail segment net sales grew 2.0% to $252.7 million while the segment’s sales volume, measured in pounds shipped, increased 0.4%. Bachan’s accounted for 720 basis points of Retail segment net sales growth and 750 basis points of Retail segment volume growth. The impact of the cyclospora outbreak is estimated to have reduced Retail segment net sales by approximately 4%. In the Foodservice segment, net sales declined 6.9% to $228.8 million while the segment’s sales volume, measured in pounds shipped, fell 7.6%. Excluding the non-core TSA sales, Foodservice segment net sales decreased 2.6% while the segment’s core sales volume declined 4.1%. The impact of the cyclospora outbreak is estimated to have reduced Foodservice segment net sales by approximately 2%.
Consolidated gross profit decreased $8.0 million to $110.8 million driven by the sales decline and associated impact of lower production volumes on our plant operations; higher freight costs; and costs for the addition of new production capacity at our sauce and dressing facility in Atlanta, Georgia.

SG&A expenses increased $10.9 million to $69.3 million. In addition to the incremental core SG&A expenses attributed to Bachan’s, the higher SG&A costs also include $2.4 million in noncash amortization expense attributed to Bachan’s intangible assets.
In the prior-year quarter, restructuring and impairment charges of $1.1 million are attributed to the closure of our sauce and dressing facility in Milpitas, California.
Consolidated operating income decreased $17.8 million to $41.4 million. Excluding the $2.4 million in noncash amortization expense for Bachan’s intangible assets and last year’s restructuring and impairment charges of $1.1 million, Adjusted Operating Income declined $16.6 million to $43.9 million. The decrease in Adjusted Operating Income reflects the lower gross profit and higher SG&A expenses.
Interest expense totaled $2.8 million in the current year versus no interest expense last year, as a portion of the Bachan’s acquisition purchase price was financed with a $200 million term loan.
Net income decreased $17.9 million to $29.3 million, or $1.07 per diluted share, versus $47.2 million, or $1.71 per diluted share, last year. In the current-year quarter, the incremental noncash amortization expense for the Bachan’s intangible assets decreased net income by $1.9 million, or $0.07 per diluted share. In the prior-year quarter, restructuring and impairment charges reduced net income by $0.9 million, or $0.03 per diluted share. Resulting Adjusted Diluted EPS was $1.14 in the current-year quarter versus $1.74 last year.

Reconciliation of GAAP to non-GAAP Financial Measures
The Marzetti Company prepares its consolidated financial statements in accordance with U.S. Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (“GAAP”). However, from time to time, the corporation may present in its public statements, press releases and SEC filings, non-GAAP financial measures such as Adjusted Consolidated Net Sales, Adjusted Foodservice Net Sales, Adjusted Cost of Sales, Adjusted Gross Profit, Adjusted Gross Margin, Adjusted Operating Income, and Adjusted Net Income Per Diluted Share (“Adjusted Diluted EPS”). Management considers such non-GAAP financial measures to provide useful supplemental information to investors in facilitating year-over-year comparisons by removing non-recurring items or other items that management believes do not directly reflect the underlying operations. Management uses these non-GAAP measures in the preparation of our annual operating plan and for our monthly analysis of operating results. Reconciliations of the non-GAAP measures to the most comparable GAAP financial measures are provided below. The corporation’s definitions of these non-GAAP measures may differ from similarly titled measures used by other companies. These non-GAAP measures should be considered supplemental to, and not a substitute for, financial information prepared in accordance with GAAP.
Adjusted Consolidated Net Sales, Adjusted Foodservice Net Sales, Adjusted Cost of Sales, Adjusted Gross Profit and Adjusted Gross Margin are non-GAAP financial measures that exclude non-core sales and cost of sales attributed to a temporary supply agreement (“TSA”) made in connection with our February 2025 acquisition of Winland’s Atlanta-based sauce and dressing production facility. The TSA sales are included in the reported net sales for our Foodservice segment and did not contribute meaningfully to gross profit. The TSA sales commenced in March 2025 and concluded during the quarter ended March 31, 2026.
