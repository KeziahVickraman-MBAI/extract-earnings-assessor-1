The Ensign Group Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results;
Raises 2026 Annual Earnings Guidance

Conference Call and Webcast scheduled for tomorrow, October 28, 2026 at 10:00 am PT

SAN JUAN CAPISTRANO, California – October 27, 2026 – The Ensign Group, Inc. (Nasdaq: ENSG), the parent company of the Ensign(TM) group of companies, which provide post-acute healthcare services and invest in the long-term healthcare industry, primarily in skilled nursing and senior living facilities, announced operating results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, reporting GAAP diluted earnings per share of $1.76 and adjusted earnings per share(2) of $2.03.

"We are thrilled to report another outstanding quarter. Our teams continue to demonstrate their unwavering commitment to dignifying post-acute care through moments of truth. Our core values, which our teams refer to using the acronym CAPLICO, create a shared culture that empowers nearly 60,000 partners to lead with compassion, accountability, ownership, and a relentless commitment to excellence. We believe culture is not separate from performance, it is the foundation that makes sustainable clinical, operational and financial performance possible,” said Barry Port, Chief Executive Officer of The Ensign Group.

"Our clinical leaders also continue to drive outstanding outcomes, which is particularly impressive given our growth over the past several years. According to the most recently published CMS data, our Same Facilities achieved annual survey results that were 15% better at the state level and 28% better at the county level. This is especially notable given that many of these facilities were 1- and 2-star buildings at acquisition. Additionally, our Same Facilities outperformed in 5-Star Quality Measure results by 21% at the state level. These results reinforce our position as the provider of choice in our markets and demonstrate our ability to create long-term value through sustained clinical excellence,” Port said.

Clinical Highlights(1):
▪Same Facilities achieved Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) Quality Measure ratings that were 21% better than industry peers in our operating states, demonstrating superior clinical outcomes.
▪Same Facilities achieved CMS Cycle 1 survey inspection results that were 15% better than industry peers in our operating states, validating clinical excellence through regulatory oversight.
▪Over 80% of our skilled nursing operations earned a CMS Quality Measure rating of 4 or 5 stars, demonstrating our continued commitment to delivering high-quality clinical care.
▪Short-stay residents achieved functional improvement rates 30% better than industry peers in our operating states, reflecting our focus on rehabilitation and helping patients regain independence.
▪Long-stay residents had antipsychotic medication rates 28% better than industry peers in our operating states, reflecting our focus on appropriate medication management and resident-centered care.
▪Administrator turnover for Same Facilities was 45% lower than our industry peers in our operating states, supporting leadership continuity and operational stability.
▪12% of all our eligible operations, comprised of 40 buildings in 8 different states, earned CMS High Performing designations under its new risk-based survey program, placing them among a select group of facilities recognized by CMS for consistently strong performance.
▪43 Ensign-affiliated operations were recognized on Newsweek’s America’s Best Nursing Homes 2027 list.
Financial Highlights:

▪GAAP diluted earnings per share for the quarter was $1.76, an increase of 23.9% over the prior year quarter, and adjusted diluted earnings per share(2) for the quarter was $2.03, an increase of 23.8% over the prior year quarter.


▪GAAP net income was $104.3 million for the quarter, an increase of 24.4% over the prior year quarter, and adjusted net income(2) was $120.5 million for the quarter, an increase of 24.9% over the prior year quarter.
▪Same Facilities and Transitioning Facilities occupancy for the quarter were 84.1% and 86.1%, an increase of 1.6% and 3.9%, respectively, over the prior year quarter.
▪Same Facilities and Transitioning Facilities skilled mix revenue for the quarter increased by 10.7% and 10.5%, respectively, over the prior year quarter and skilled days for the quarter increased by 7.2% and 7.9%, respectively, over the prior year quarter.
▪Same Facilities and Transitioning Facilities Medicare revenue for the quarter improved by 11.1% and 7.8%, respectively, from the prior year quarter and Medicare days for the quarter improved by 7.6% and 6.3%, respectively, from the prior year quarter.
▪Same Facilities and Transitioning Facilities managed care revenue for the quarter improved by 9.1% and 7.2%, respectively, from the prior year quarter and managed care days for the quarter improved by 3.1% and 3.8%, respectively, from the prior year quarter.
▪Consolidated revenue for the quarter was $1.48 billion, an increase of 14.5% over the prior year quarter.
▪Standard Bearer(3) revenue was $47.7 million for the quarter, an increase of 46.3% over the prior year quarter. FFO was $27.3 million for the quarter, an increase of 41.3% over the prior year quarter.

Clinical and Operating Results
“We continue to see strong demand across our portfolio and experienced growth in occupancy and skilled mix during the quarter. In particular, we saw year-over-year growth across all skilled payor categories during the quarter, with growth in Medicare being particularly strong. On the census front, our Same Facilities and Transitioning occupancy for the third quarter was 84.1% and 86.1%, respectively. In addition to growing census, our Same Facilities and Transitioning Facilities skilled mix revenue increased by 10.7% and 10.5%, respectively, over the prior year quarter. Medicare revenue increased for both our Same Facilities and Transitioning Facilities by 11.1% and 7.8%, respectively. Also, managed care revenue increased by 9.1% and 7.2%, respectively, for Same Facilities and Transitioning Facilities over the prior year quarter, with total skilled days up 7.2% and 7.9%, respectively, from the prior year quarter,” Port said.

He added, “These improvements in occupancy and skilled mix clearly demonstrate that patients, families, hospital systems, physicians, and managed care organizations continue to choose Ensign affiliated operations because of the outcomes our teams achieve and the distinctive culture and experience our local teams create every day. This cannot and will not happen, especially consistently and over a long period of time, without consistently achieving these industry-leading high-quality clinical outcomes. Occupancy and skilled mix are more than financial metrics; they are one of the clearest external validations that an operation consistently delivers the outcomes and experience that stakeholders value."

“Due to the strength of the third quarter and our confidence in the fourth quarter, we are increasing our annual 2026 earnings guidance to $7.94 to $8.02 per diluted share, up from our original guidance of $7.75 to $7.85. We are also raising the lower end of our annual revenue guidance to $5.89 billion, while maintaining the upper end at $5.92 billion. The midpoint of our earnings guidance represents a 21.5% increase over 2025 and 45.5% growth over 2024," Mr. Port said.

Speaking to the Company’s acquisition growth, Chad Keetch, Ensign’s Chief Investment Officer and Executive Vice President said, “We continue to acquire new operations with significant long-term upside and expect to maintain a healthy pace of growth as we expand our mission driven approach to transform and dignify post-acute care. During the quarter and since, we accelerated our growth by adding 22 new operations, including three real estate assets and 19 leased operations. We also acquired five real estate properties that were under lease arrangements that we are operating. Since 2024, we have successfully sourced, underwritten, closed, and transitioned 122 new operations across several markets, many of which are already performing at or above expectations, both clinically and financially. We continue to see opportunities that include everything from larger portfolios, landlords looking to replace current tenants, non-profits looking to divest of their post-acute assets and a steady flow of traditional one offs. We have several new additions lining up for next year as our local leadership and their partners at the Service Center work together to source, underwrite and carefully select the right opportunities.”



Suzanne Snapper, Ensign’s Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer reported that the Company’s liquidity remains strong with approximately $337.6 million of cash on hand and $731.6 million of available capacity under its line-of-credit. Ms. Snapper also indicated that, “Management’s annual guidance is based on diluted weighted average common shares outstanding of approximately 59.5 million and a 25.0% tax rate. In addition, the guidance assumes, among other things, normalized insurance costs, acquisitions expected to close through the fourth quarter of 2026 and management’s current expectations regarding reimbursement rates. It also excludes certain charges that arise outside the normal course of business, amortization of system implementation costs, acquisition related costs and share-based compensation.”

Florida represents a natural continuation of our strategic expansion into the Southeast, which began with our entry into Tennessee, South Carolina and Alabama. Our approach has been deliberate: enter new markets, develop strong local leadership and clusters, and build the infrastructure necessary to support disciplined growth over time," Mr. Keetch said.

Standard Bearer announced the following real estate acquisitions, which are operated by an Ensign-affiliate:
•Las Ventanas de Socorro, a 126-bed skilled nursing facility located in Socorro, Texas;
•Los Arcos del Norte Care Center, a 124-bed skilled nursing facility located in El Paso, Texas; and
• Englewood Heights Nursing and Rehabilitation, a 118-bed skilled nursing facility located in Pensacola, Florida.
Additionally, Standard Bearer announced it acquired the real estate assets of five facilities that were previously operated under third-party triple net leases. These facilities total 755 skilled nursing beds and are now subject to long-term leases with subsidiaries of Standard Bearer.
Ensign's growing portfolio consists of 418 healthcare operations, 34 of which also include senior living operations, across 18 states. Ensign now owns 189 real estate assets, 150 of which are operated by an Ensign affiliate. Mr. Keetch noted that Ensign’s overall strategy will continue to include both leasing and acquiring the real estate, and that the Company is actively looking for performing and underperforming operations in several states.
The Company continues to provide additional disclosure on Standard Bearer, which is comprised of 183 owned properties. Of these assets, 146 are leased to Ensign-affiliated operators and 38 are leased to third-party operators. Mr. Keetch noted that each of these properties are subject to triple-net, long-term leases and generated rental revenue of $47.7 million for the quarter, of which $42.1 million was derived from Ensign affiliated operations. For the quarter, Ensign reported $27.3 million in FFO.
The Company also paid a quarterly cash dividend of $0.065 per share of Ensign common stock. Ms. Snapper noted that as the Company’s liquidity remains strong, it plans to continue its long history of paying dividends into the future.

About Ensign™

The Ensign Group, Inc.'s independent subsidiaries provide a broad spectrum of skilled nursing and senior living services, physical, occupational and speech therapies and other rehabilitative and healthcare services at 418 healthcare facilities in Alabama, Alaska, Arizona, California, Colorado, Florida, Idaho, Iowa, Kansas, Nebraska, Nevada, Oregon, South Carolina, Tennessee, Texas, Utah, Washington and Wisconsin. As part of its investment strategy, the Company will also acquire, lease and own healthcare real estate to service the post-acute care continuum through acquisition and investment opportunities in healthcare properties. Ensign’s new business venture operating subsidiaries also offer several other post-acute-related services, including mobile x-ray, emergency and non-emergency transportation services, long-term care pharmacy and other consulting services also across several states. Each of these operations is operated by a separate, independent subsidiary that has its own management, employees and assets. Discussion of Non-GAAP Financial Measures

Adjusted EBT consists of net income before (a) provision for income taxes, (b) stock-based compensation expense, (c) acquisition related costs, (d) costs incurred related to system implementations, (e) (gain) loss on long-lived assets and business interruption recoveries, (f) write off of deferred financing fees, (g) litigation arising outside of the ordinary course of business, (h) gain on other investments, and (i) amortization of patient base intangible assets. Adjusted net income consists of net income excluding (a) stock‑based compensation expense, (b) acquisition related costs, (c) costs incurred related to system implementations, (d) (gain) loss on long-lived assets and business interruption recoveries, (e) write off of deferred financing fees, (f) litigation arising outside of the ordinary course of business, (g) gain on other investments, (h) amortization of patient base intangible assets, and (i) the income tax effect of these adjustments. Adjusted earnings per share consists of adjusted net income divided by the weighted‑average diluted shares outstanding for the applicable period. EBITDA consists of net income before (a) interest income, (b) provision for income taxes, (c) depreciation and amortization and (d) interest expense. Adjusted EBITDA consists of net income before (a) interest income, (b) provision for income taxes, (c) depreciation and amortization, (d) interest expense, (e) stock-based compensation expense, (f) acquisition related costs, (g) costs incurred related to system implementations, (h) (gain) loss on long-lived assets and business interruption recoveries, (i) litigation arising outside of the ordinary course of business and (j) gain on other investments. Adjusted EBITDAR consists of net income before (a) interest income, (b) provision for income taxes, (c) depreciation and amortization, (d) interest expense, (e) rent-cost of services, (f) stock-based compensation expense, (g) acquisition related costs, (h) costs incurred related to system implementations, (i) (gain) loss on long-lived assets and business interruption recoveries (j) litigation arising outside of the ordinary course of business and (k) gain on other investments. Funds from Operations (FFO) for our Standard Bearer segment consists of segment income, excluding depreciation and amortization related to real estate, gains or losses from the sale of real estate, insurance recoveries related to real estate and impairment of long-lived assets. The Company believes that the presentation of adjusted EBT, adjusted net income, adjusted earnings per share, EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA and FFO provides important supplemental information to management and investors to evaluate the Company’s operating performance. Adjusted EBITDAR is a financial valuation measure that is not specified in GAAP. This measure is not displayed as a performance measure as it excludes rent expense, which is a normal and recurring operating expense. The Company believes disclosure of adjusted EBT, adjusted net income, adjusted earnings per share, EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA, adjusted EBITDAR and FFO has substance because the excluded revenues and expenses are infrequent in nature and are variable in nature, or do not represent current revenues or cash expenditures. A material limitation associated with the use of these measures as compared to the GAAP measures of net income and diluted earnings per share is that they may not be comparable with the calculation of net income and diluted earnings per share for other companies in the Company's industry. These non-GAAP financial measures should not be relied upon to the exclusion of GAAP financial measures. For further information regarding why the Company believes that these non-GAAP measures provide useful information to investors, the specific manner in which management uses these measures, and some of the limitations associated with the use of these measures, please refer to the Company's periodic filings with the Securities and Exchange Commission, including its Annual Report on Form 10-K and Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q. The Company’s periodic filings are available on the SEC's website at www.sec.gov or under the "Financials" link of the Investor Relations section on Ensign’s website at http://www.ensigngroup.net.
