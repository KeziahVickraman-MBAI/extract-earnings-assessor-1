Energy Focus, Inc. Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
SOLON, Ohio, November 10, 2026 -- Energy Focus, Inc. (NASDAQ:EFOI) (the “Company” or “Energy Focus”), a leader in sustainable, energy-efficient lighting and control system products for the commercial market and military maritime market (“MMM”), today announced its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights:
•Net sales of $4.8 million, an increase of 481.2% compared with the third quarter of 2025, reflecting an increase of $4.3 million, or 2115.8% in commercial sales period-over-period. Sequentially, net sales increased 28.1% compared with the second quarter of 2026, primarily reflecting increases of approximately $2.2 million in commercial sales, partially offset by a decrease of $1.4 million in military sales. The net sales increase in commercial sales was primarily driven by the expansion of the Company's Uninterruptible Power Supply (“UPS”) business with a new customer in Taiwan during the third quarter of 2026, including related installation and setup services.
•Gross profit margin was 20.5% in the third quarter of 2026, compared with 17.8% in the third quarter of 2025 and (6.8)% in the second quarter of 2026. The increase was primarily driven by higher commercial sales resulting from the expansion of the Company's UPS business with a new customer in Taiwan, as well as a more favorable product mix. In addition, during the third quarter of 2026, the Company received a refund of approximately $184 thousand from U.S. Customs and Border Protection ("CBP") related to customs duties previously paid, which was recorded as a reduction to cost of goods sold and positively impacted gross profit.
•Income from operations of $0.5 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared with a loss from operations of $0.2 million in the third quarter of 2025 and a loss from operations of $0.9 million in the second quarter of 2026. The increase was primarily driven by higher commercial sales resulting from the expansion of the Company's UPS business with a new customer in Taiwan.
•Net income was $0.5 million, or $0.08 per basic and diluted share of common stock, in the third quarter of 2026, compared with a net loss of $0.2 million, or $(0.03) per basic and diluted share, in the third quarter of 2025 and net loss of $0.9 million, or $(0.14) per basic and diluted share, in the second quarter of 2026.
•Cash was $3.3 million as of September 30, 2026, compared with $1.1 million and $0.9 million as of December 31, 2025 and September 30, 2025, respectively. The increase was primarily due to proceeds of $0.9 million from short-term borrowings and the timing difference between customer collections and payments to suppliers, as certain customer payments had been received while the related supplier payments remained outstanding as of September 30, 2026.

About Energy Focus
Energy Focus is a leader in energy-efficient light-emitting diode (“LED”) lighting and energy infrastructure solutions. As the creator of the first flicker-free LED lamps, Energy Focus develops high quality LED lighting products and controls that provide extensive energy and maintenance savings, as well as aesthetics, safety, health and sustainability benefits over conventional lighting. Energy Focus is headquartered in Solon, Ohio. For more information, visit our website at www.energyfocus.com. The Company routinely posts important updates on its website.

Non-GAAP Measures
In addition to the results in this release that are presented in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles in the United States (“U.S. GAAP”), we provide certain non-GAAP measures, which present operating results on an adjusted basis. These non-GAAP measures are supplemental measures of performance that are not required by or presented in accordance with U.S. GAAP, and include:
•adjusted EBITDA, which we define as net income (loss) before giving effect to financing charges, income taxes, non-cash depreciation, non-cash stock-based compensation, accrued incentive compensation, non-routine charges to other income or expense; and
•adjusted gross margins, which we define as our gross profit margins excluding the impact of inventory reserve charges (excess and obsolete, in-transit and net realizable value adjustments) and inventory write-offs. Management believes this measure better reflects the underlying profitability of products sold during the period by excluding the impact of prior period inventory purchasing decisions.
We believe that our use of these non-GAAP financial measures permits investors to assess the operating performance of our business relative to our performance based on U.S. GAAP results and relative to other companies within the industry by isolating the effects of items that may vary from period to period without correlation to core operating performance or that vary widely among similar companies, and to assess liquidity, cash flow performance of the operations, and the product margins of our business relative to our U.S. GAAP results and relative to other companies in the industry by isolating the effects of certain items that do not have a current period impact. However, our presentation of these non-GAAP measures should not be construed as an indication that our future results will be unaffected by unusual or infrequent items or that the items for which we have made adjustments are unusual or infrequent or will not recur. Further, there are limitations on the use of these non-GAAP measures to compare our results to other companies within the industry because they are not necessarily standardized or comparable to similarly titled measures used by other companies. We believe that the disclosure of these non-GAAP measures is useful to investors as they form part of the basis for how our management team and Board of Directors evaluate our operating performance.
Adjusted EBITDA and adjusted gross margins do not represent cash generated from operating activities in accordance with U.S. GAAP, are not necessarily indicative of cash available to fund cash needs and are not intended to and should not be considered as alternatives to cash flow, net income and gross profit margins, respectively, computed in accordance with U.S. GAAP as measures of liquidity or operating performance.
