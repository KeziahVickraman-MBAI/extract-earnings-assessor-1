CAMBRIDGE, Mass.--(BUSINESS WIRE)— November 10, 2026 — Scholar Rock (NASDAQ: SRRK), a global biopharmaceutical company dedicated to improving the lives of patients with rare, severe, and debilitating neuromuscular diseases by applying its leading platform in myostatin biology, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided an update on recent company developments.

“The third quarter was a defining period for Scholar Rock, marked by the FDA approval and U.S. launch of ISEMBYLD, the world’s first and only muscle-targeted therapy for children and adults living with SMA,” said David L. Hallal, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Scholar Rock. “We are very encouraged by the early ISEMBYLD launch dynamics in the U.S., where the breadth of early demand across SMA treatment centers, physicians, and patients reinforces the significant unmet need in the SMA community. With the U.S. launch underway, our regulatory applications advancing in Europe and Japan, important progress across our world-leading anti-myostatin pipeline, and our strengthened financial position, we are building a foundation to redefine the treatment of neuromuscular disease globally and drive sustainable growth for years to come.”

 

Business Highlights and Upcoming Milestones

Apitegromab Spinal Muscular Atrophy (SMA) Program

•
ISEMBYLD (apitegromab-mstn) approved by FDA on September 11, 2026. ISEMBYLD is the first and only muscle-targeted therapy for SMA and is indicated for adults and children two years of age and older who are currently receiving a survival motor neuron 2 (SMN2)-targeted

 

 

treatment. In the Phase 3 pivotal, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled SAPPHIRE study, individuals receiving ISEMBYLD with an SMN2-targeted treatment demonstrated a robust, clinically meaningful improvement in motor function at one year while those on an SMN2-targeted therapy alone experienced a loss of motor function.
•
U.S. launch of ISEMBYLD underway with strong early patient and physician demand. The U.S. team is in the field engaging with physicians, SMA care teams, and patients as well as national and regional payers. ISEMBYLD vials were labeled, packaged, and shipped to the specialty network within one week of FDA approval. A J-code application was submitted to the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) in September for an ISEMBYLD-specific J-code on April 1, 2027. Encouraging early, broad-based demand for ISEMBYLD is reflected in the breadth of prescribing physicians and enrolling patients. Early launch dynamics are reinforcing the significant opportunity to serve children and adults with SMA in the U.S.
•
Apitegromab application in Europe on track for H1 2027 Committee for Medicinal Products for Human Use (CHMP) opinion. In alignment with guidance from the European Medicines Agency (EMA), Scholar Rock resubmitted the apitegromab Marketing Authorisation Application (MAA) for the treatment of children and adults living with SMA in October 2026. Following validation of the submission, review of the MAA is underway, and the Company expects a CHMP opinion in the first half of 2027. The resubmitted MAA includes Scholar Rock’s alternate fill-finish facility in place of the Catalent Indiana LLC fill-finish facility. The alternate fill-finish facility is the same site that supported the U.S. FDA approval of ISEMBYLD in September 2026. As the regulatory review progresses, the Scholar Rock team in Europe continues to engage with key stakeholders on SMA disease awareness and education initiatives.
•
Apitegromab Japanese New Drug Application (JNDA) submitted in Japan and accepted under priority review. Scholar Rock submitted the apitegromab JNDA in Japan, which was accepted under priority review. For priority review applications, Japan’s Pharmaceuticals and Medical Devices Agency (PMDA) has typically targeted a review cycle of approximately nine months. In addition, apitegromab for the treatment of people living with SMA was granted an Orphan Drug Designation.
•
Regulatory engagement for subcutaneous apitegromab program to progress. Scholar Rock has developed a high concentration subcutaneous formulation of apitegromab. Following the September 11, 2026 FDA approval of ISEMBYLD, the Company is engaging with regulators on the development path for this formulation. Subcutaneous apitegromab is designed to provide optionality for the SMA community as a small volume, self- or caregiver-administered anti-myostatin antibody.
•
Phase 2 OPAL trial advancing. Enrollment is well advanced and dosing is ongoing (NCT07047144). The OPAL study is evaluating apitegromab in infants and toddlers with SMA under two years of age who have received an approved SMN1-targeted gene therapy or who are receiving ongoing treatment with an approved SMN2-targeted therapy.

 

Apitegromab Facioscapulohumeral Muscular Dystrophy (FSHD) Program

•
Enrollment and dosing in Phase 2 FORGE trial ongoing. The Phase 2 randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial, called FORGE, is expected to enroll approximately 60 patients with FSHD who will be randomized 1:1 to receive either apitegromab 10mg/kg IV or placebo every 4 weeks for 52 weeks. The primary endpoint is mean lean muscle volume (LMV) change from baseline at 12 months. Other endpoints include safety, pharmacokinetics/ pharmacodynamics,

 

 

mean LMV change from baseline at 6 months, as well as several exploratory functional endpoints. In September 2026, the FDA granted Fast Track and Orphan Drug designations to apitegromab for the treatment of people living with FSHD.

SRK-439 Program

•
Phase 1 study in healthy volunteers ongoing. A Phase 1 study evaluating SRK-439 in healthy volunteers is progressing well. Scholar Rock expects to have topline data by year end 2026 and plans to share data from this study in early 2027. SRK-439 is a novel, investigational, subcutaneously administered myostatin inhibitor that binds to pro- and latent myostatin with high affinity and selectivity. Based on non-clinical data, SRK-439 has the potential to potently inhibit myostatin and increase muscle mass.

 

Financial Update

•
Definitive agreement to sell Rare Pediatric Disease Priority Review Voucher (PRV) for $205 million. In November, Scholar Rock entered into a definitive agreement to sell its PRV for $205 million. The closing of the PRV transaction is subject to customary closing conditions, including the expiration of the applicable waiting period under the Hart-Scott-Rodino (HSR) Antitrust Improvements Act. The cash will further strengthen the Company’s balance sheet with non-dilutive capital. The PRV was awarded following the September 2026 FDA approval of ISEMBYLD.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

Scholar Rock reported a net loss of $107.2 million, including stock-based compensation of $17.9 million, for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to a net loss of $102.2 million, including stock-based compensation of $18.3 million, for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. Net loss per common share was $0.81 for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $0.90 per common share for the quarter ended September 30, 2025.

•
The Company recorded net product revenue of $0.6 million from sales of ISEMBYLD in the U.S. for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. The Company did not record any revenue for the quarter ended September 30, 2025.

 

•
Cost of revenue was immaterial for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. Prior to the FDA approval of ISEMBYLD, product costs were expensed as research and development expense. The Company did not record any cost of revenue for the quarter ended September 30, 2025.
•
Research and development expense was $54.1 million, including $6.4 million in stock-based compensation, for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $50.5 million, including $5.5 million in stock-based compensation, for the quarter ended September 30, 2025.
•
Selling, general and administrative expense was $52.5 million, including $11.5 million in stock-based compensation, for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to $53.1 million, including $12.8 million in stock-based compensation, for the quarter ended September 30, 2025.
•
As of September 30, 2026, Scholar Rock had cash, cash equivalents, and marketable securities of $413.6 million.

About Scholar Rock

Scholar Rock is delivering muscle-targeted breakthroughs to transform the treatment of spinal muscular atrophy (SMA) and other rare neuromuscular diseases where muscle atrophy remains a critical unmet need. Following FDA approval in September 2026, Scholar Rock is commercializing ISEMBLYD (apitegromab-mstn) in the U.S. for people living with SMA who are 2 years of age and older and currently receiving a survival motor neuron 2 (SMN2)-targeted treatment. The Company plans to expand access to ISEMBYLD globally, in up to 50 countries worldwide, subject to regulatory approvals.

As a global leader in myostatin biology, a field focused on proteins that regulate muscle mass, the biopharmaceutical company is named for the visual resemblance of a scholar rock to protein structures. Our commitment to unlock fundamentally different treatment approaches is powered by broad application of a proprietary platform, which has developed novel monoclonal antibodies to modulate protein growth factors with extraordinary selectivity. Scholar Rock works every day to create new possibilities for patients through its highly innovative anti-myostatin program, including opportunities in additional rare neuromuscular diseases. Learn more about our neuromuscular franchise at ScholarRock.com and follow @ScholarRock on X and on LinkedIn.
