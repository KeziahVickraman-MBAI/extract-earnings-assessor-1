Tompkins Financial Corporation Reports Financial Results for Third Quarter of 2026

ITHACA, NY - Tompkins Financial Corporation (NYSE American: TMP)
Tompkins Financial Corporation ("Tompkins" or the "Company") reported diluted earnings per share of $2.00 for the third quarter of 2026, up $0.35 or 21.2%, from the third quarter of 2025 and down $0.04 per share or 2.0%, from the second quarter of 2026. Net income for the third quarter of 2026 was $28.7 million, an increase of $5.0 million, or 21.0%, from the third quarter of 2025 and down $650,000, or 2.2%, from the prior quarter.

For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, diluted earnings per share were $5.86, up $1.34 or 29.6%, from $4.52 for the same period in 2025. Net income for the year-to-date period totaled $84.0 million, up $19.2 million, or 29.6%, from $64.8 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.

Tompkins President and CEO, Stephen Romaine, commented, "We are pleased to report strong financial results for the third quarter. Our consistent levels of loan and deposit growth along with further expansion in net interest margin continue to provide momentum as we look forward. Given our strength in earnings and capital position we have approved another dividend increase, payable in the fourth quarter of 2026. This dividend represents a 13.8% increase compared to the dividend paid in the fourth quarter of 2025."

NET INTEREST INCOME
Net interest income was $77.3 million for the third quarter of 2026, up $3.3 million or 4.5% compared to the second quarter of 2026, and up $13.4 million or 21.0% compared to the third quarter of 2025. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, net interest income was $223.1 million, up $42.5 million or 23.5% when compared to the same period in 2025. The increase in net interest income compared to both prior year periods was due to improvement in net interest margin and growth in average loans.

Net interest margin was 3.65% for the third quarter of 2026, an increase of 7 basis points over the prior quarter, and an increase of 45 basis points compared to 3.20% for the third quarter of 2025. The increase in net interest margin, when compared to the most recent prior quarter, was primarily driven by higher yields on average interest-earning assets and growth in average loan balances. The year-over-year increase was primarily attributable to growth in average loan balances, improved yields on average interest-earning assets, and lower funding costs. The average yield on securities for the third quarter of 2026 increased 95 basis points over the third quarter of 2025, while the average yield on interest-earning assets increased 28 basis points over the same period.

Average loans for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 increased $119.1 million, or 1.8% (7.3% annualized), from the prior quarter and $428.0 million, or 6.9%, over the third quarter of 2025. The increase in average loans across both prior periods was primarily driven by growth in the commercial real estate and commercial and industrial portfolios. The average yield on total loans and leases was 5.60% for the third quarter of 2026, up 5 basis points from the prior quarter and up 8 basis points from the third quarter of 2025.

Average total deposits of $7.2 billion for the third quarter of 2026 increased $180.8 million, or 2.6%, over the second quarter of 2026 and $361.4 million, or 5.3%, over the third quarter of 2025. The cost of interest-bearing deposits of 2.10% for the third quarter of 2026 was up 3 basis points from the prior quarter and down 16 basis points compared to the third quarter of 2025. Average noninterest-bearing deposits totaled $2.0 billion, increasing $90.5 million, or 4.8%, over the second quarter of 2026, and $89.2 million or 4.7%, over the third quarter of 2025. The ratio of average noninterest-bearing deposits to average total deposits was 27.5% for the third quarter of 2026, up from 26.9% for the second quarter of 2026, and consistent with 27.7% for the third quarter of 2025. The average cost of interest-bearing liabilities was 2.24% for the third quarter of 2026, unchanged from the prior quarter, and down 21 basis points compared to the third quarter of 2025.

NONINTEREST INCOME
Noninterest income totaled $12.6 million for the third quarter of 2026, down $11.0 million, or 46.7%, from the third quarter of 2025. The decrease was primarily attributable to an $11.3 million decline in insurance revenue
2

resulting from the sale of our insurance subsidiary, Tompkins Insurance Agencies, Inc. ("TIA"), in the fourth quarter of 2025. Partially offsetting this decline were increases in fee-based service income, including increases in wealth management fees of $337,000 or 6.8%, service charges on deposit accounts of $164,000 or 8.9%, and card service income of $74,000 or 2.6%.

Year-to-date noninterest income of $37.5 million was down $33.6 million, or 47.2%, from the same period in 2025. Contributing to the year-over-year decrease was a $32.5 million decline in insurance revenue due to the sale of TIA, as well as a $2.2 million, or 23.2%, decrease in other income, primarily reflecting a gain on the sale of other real estate owned recognized in the second quarter of 2025. Partially offsetting these declines were increases in fee-based service income, including increases in wealth management fees of $749,000 or 5.0%, service charges on deposit accounts of $180,000 or 3.3%, and card service income of $236,000 or 2.7%.

NONINTEREST EXPENSE
Noninterest expense totaled $49.1 million for the third quarter of 2026, down $4.8 million, or 8.8%, from the third quarter of 2025. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, noninterest expense totaled $143.9 million, down $12.2 million, or 7.8%, from the same period in 2025.

The decrease in noninterest expense for both periods was primarily attributable to the sale of TIA in the fourth quarter of 2025. The third quarter and year-to-date periods in 2025 included TIA-related salaries and wages and other employee benefits expenses of $6.1 million and $18.2 million, respectively, as well as other noninterest expenses of $1.4 million and $4.3 million, respectively. As a result, salaries and wages and other employee benefits decreased $3.8 million, or 11.3%, and $11.8 million, or 11.9%, respectively, for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, respectively, compared to the same periods in the prior year. These decreases were partially offset by annual merit increases and higher other employee benefit costs, including health insurance, as well as increases in technology expense.

INCOME TAX EXPENSE
Provision for income tax expense was $9.1 million, for an effective rate of 24.1%, for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $9.2 million, for an effective rate of 24.0%, for the most recent prior quarter, and $7.4 million, for an effective rate of 23.9% for the third quarter of 2025.

For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, provision for income tax expense was $26.7 million, for an effective tax rate of 24.1%, compared to $20.3 million for an effective tax rate of 23.9%, for the same period in 2025.

ASSET QUALITY
The allowance for credit losses was 0.90% of total loans and leases at September 30, 2026, up from 0.89% at June 30, 2026, and down from 0.95% at September 30, 2025. The decrease in the allowance for credit losses coverage ratio compared to September 30, 2025 was mainly due to improved economic forecasts for unemployment and gross domestic product and lower qualitative reserves related to asset quality. These were partially offset by additional specific reserves on individually analyzed nonaccrual commercial credits. The ratio of the allowance to total nonperforming loans and leases was 113.12% at September 30, 2026, compared to 111.29% at June 30, 2026, and 113.06% at September 30, 2025.

Provision for credit losses for the third quarter of 2026 was $3.0 million, up from $1.5 million for the most recent prior quarter, and up from $2.5 million for the third quarter of 2025. The increase in the provision expense compared to the most recent prior quarter was mainly driven by loan growth and specific reserves for individually evaluated loans, partially offset by updated economic forecasts. Net charge-offs for the three months ended September 30, 2026 were $1.2 million, compared to $1.6 million for the second quarter of 2026, and $1.1 million for the third quarter of 2025.

Nonperforming assets of $53.8 million represented 0.60% of total assets at September 30, 2026, compared to $52.9 million or 0.60% of total assets at June 30, 2026, and $53.0 million or 0.63% of total assets at September 30, 2025. Loans past due 30-89 days totaled $6.7 million at September 30, 2026, $4.7 million at June 30, 2026, and $7.8 million at September 30, 2025.

Special Mention and Substandard loans and leases totaled $151.1 million at September 30, 2026, compared to $140.0 million reported at June 30, 2026, and $144.2 million at September 30, 2025. Special Mention loans increased $6.8 million and Substandard loans increased by $4.3 million from the prior quarter end. These increases were primarily attributable to two commercial real estate relationships, including the downgrade of a $12.7 million credit to Special Mention and the migration of an existing $4.6 million Special Mention credit to Substandard. Management believes that the collateral securing these credits remains sufficient to mitigate potential loss exposure.

CAPITAL POSITION
Capital ratios at September 30, 2026 remained well above the regulatory minimums for well-capitalized institutions. The ratio of total capital to risk-weighted assets was 14.89% at September 30, 2026, unchanged compared to June 30, 2026, and increased compared to 13.27% at September 30, 2025. The ratio of Tier 1 capital to average assets was 10.73% at September 30, 2026, compared to 10.69% at June 30, 2026, and 9.41% at September 30, 2025.

The Company repurchased 18,118 shares of common stock at an aggregate cost of $1.8 million during the third quarter of 2026 and repurchased a total of 53,636 shares of common stock at an aggregate cost of $4.5 million
4

during the year-to-date period ending September 30, 2026. All of these shares were purchased under the Company's 2025 Stock Repurchase Plan.

LIQUIDITY POSITION
The Company's liquidity position at September 30, 2026 was consistent with its position at June 30, 2026. The Company's sources of liquidity include ready access to national and regional wholesale funding sources including Federal funds purchased, repurchase agreements, brokered deposits, Federal Reserve Bank's Discount Window advances and Federal Home Loan Bank (FHLB) advances. The Company maintained ready access to liquidity of $1.5 billion, or 17.1% of total assets, at September 30, 2026.

ABOUT TOMPKINS FINANCIAL CORPORATION
Tompkins Financial Corporation is a banking and financial services company serving the Central, Western, and Hudson Valley regions of New York and the Southeastern region of Pennsylvania. Headquartered in Ithaca, NY, Tompkins Financial is parent to Tompkins Bank & Trust, which offers a full array of products and services, including commercial and consumer banking. Tompkins Bank & Trust provides wealth management services under the Tompkins Financial Advisors brand, including investment management, trust and estate, financial and tax planning services.
