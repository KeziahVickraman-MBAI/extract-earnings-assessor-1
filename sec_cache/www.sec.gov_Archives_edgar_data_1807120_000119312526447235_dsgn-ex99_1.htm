Design Therapeutics Announces Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Recent Business Updates

DT-216P2 Anticipated Milestones: Registrational Plan Update in Q4 2026 and 12-Week RESTORE-FA Data in Q1 2027

Cash and securities of $208.6 million at quarter-end provide strong financial runway

 

Carlsbad, Calif., November 4, 2026 - Design Therapeutics, Inc. (Nasdaq: DSGN), a clinical-stage biotechnology company developing treatments for serious degenerative genetic diseases, today reported third quarter 2026 financial results and highlighted business updates and upcoming milestones across its GeneTAC® portfolio.

 

“We remain focused on evaluating DT-216P2 over longer treatment durations and preparing for the next stage of clinical development in Friedreich ataxia,” said Pratik Shah, Ph.D., chairperson and chief executive officer of Design Therapeutics. “In DM1, our Phase 1 trial of DT-818 is evaluating an approach designed to selectively reduce transcription of the mutant DMPK allele, targeting the source of toxic RNA. Clinical readouts expected in 2027 will help guide these programs as we pursue our vision for the GeneTAC® platform: to address the underlying causes of serious genetic diseases and deliver meaningful benefits for patients.”

 

Corporate Highlights

•
Friedreich ataxia (FA): The Phase 1/2 multiple ascending dose RESTORE-FA trial of DT-216P2 is ongoing. Design expects to provide an update on its registrational plans in the fourth quarter of 2026, with data following 12 weeks of treatment expected in the first quarter of 2027.
•
Myotonic Dystrophy Type-1 (DM1): The Phase 1 multiple-ascending dose (MAD) trial of DT-818, a GeneTAC® small molecule designed to selectively reduce transcription of the mutant DMPK allele in patients with DM1, is ongoing. Design anticipates reporting data from this trial in 2027.
•
Fuchs Endothelial Corneal Dystrophy (FECD): A Phase 2 biomarker trial of DT-168 is ongoing to evaluate safety, tolerability and corneal endothelium biomarkers in FECD patients who are scheduled for corneal transplant surgery, with data expected in 2027.
•
Huntington’s disease (HD): Design continues to advance preclinical characterization of several candidate molecules for its Huntington’s disease program.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

•
R&D Expenses: Research and development (R&D) expenses were $16.9 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
•
G&A Expenses: General and administrative (G&A) expenses were $5.3 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

 

•
Net Loss: Net loss was $20.3 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
•
Cash Position: Cash, cash equivalents and investment securities were $208.6 million as of September 30, 2026.

 

About Design Therapeutics

Design Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biotechnology company developing a new class of therapies based on its platform of GeneTAC® gene targeted chimera small molecules. The company’s GeneTAC® molecules are designed to either dial up or dial down the expression of a specific disease-causing gene to address the underlying cause of disease. In addition to its clinical-stage GeneTAC® programs, DT-216P2, in development for patients with Friedreich ataxia, DT-168, for Fuchs endothelial corneal dystrophy, and DT-818, for myotonic dystrophy type-1, the company is advancing a program in Huntington’s disease. Discovery efforts are underway for multiple genomic medicines. For more information, please visit designtx.com.
