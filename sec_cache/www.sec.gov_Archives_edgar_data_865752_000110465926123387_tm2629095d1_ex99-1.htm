MONSTER BEVERAGE REPORTS 2026 THIRD QUARTER FINANCIAL RESULTS

 

2026 Third Quarter Highlights

 

·Net Sales rise 16.7 percent to $2.56 billion
·Net Sales to Customers Outside the United States increase 28.1 percent to $1.20 billion, representing 47 percent of total Net Sales
·Net Income Per Diluted Share increases 7.8 percent to $0.29 per share (8.5 percent to $0.30 per share on a non-GAAP adjusted basis)1

 

1The tables at the end of this press release provide a reconciliation of non-GAAP financial measures to the Company’s results, as reported under GAAP. (See “Reconciliation of GAAP and Non-GAAP Information” below.)

 

Corona, CA – November 5, 2026 – Monster Beverage Corporation (NASDAQ: MNST) today reported financial results for the three- and nine-months ended September 30, 2026.

 

Net sales for the 2026 third quarter increased 16.7 percent to $2.56 billion, from $2.20 billion in the same period last year. Net changes in foreign currency exchange rates had a favorable impact on net sales for the 2026 third quarter of $8.1 million. Net sales on a foreign currency adjusted basis (non-GAAP) increased 16.3 percent in the 2026 third quarter.

 

Net sales, excluding the Alcohol Brands segment (non-GAAP), increased 17.1 percent in the 2026 third quarter. Net sales, excluding the Alcohol Brands segment, on a foreign currency adjusted basis (non-GAAP), increased 16.7 percent in the 2026 third quarter.

 

Net sales for the Company’s Monster Energy® Drinks segment, which primarily includes the Company’s Monster Energy® drinks, Reign Total Body Fuel® high performance energy drinks, Bang Energy® drinks, Storm™ and Reign Storm® total wellness energy drinks and FLRT™ total wellness energy drinks, increased 17.1 percent to $2.37 billion for the 2026 third quarter, from $2.03 billion for the 2025 third quarter. Net changes in foreign currency exchange rates had a favorable impact on net sales for the Monster Energy® Drinks segment of approximately $7.5 million for the 2026 third quarter. Net sales on a foreign currency adjusted basis (non-GAAP) for the Monster Energy® Drinks segment increased 16.7 percent in the 2026 third quarter.

Net sales for the Company’s Strategic Brands segment, which primarily includes the various energy drink brands acquired from The Coca-Cola Company, as well as the Company’s affordable energy brands, Predator® and Fury®, increased 19.8 percent to $156.4 million for the 2026 third quarter, from $130.5 million in the 2025 third quarter. Net changes in foreign currency exchange rates had a favorable impact on net sales for the Strategic Brands segment of approximately $0.6 million for the 2026 third quarter. Net sales on a foreign currency adjusted basis (non-GAAP) for the Strategic Brands segment increased 19.3 percent in the 2026 third quarter.

 

Net sales for the Alcohol Brands segment, which is comprised of various craft beers, flavored malt beverages and hard seltzers, decreased 13.6 percent to $28.5 million for the 2026 third quarter, from $33.0 million in the 2025 third quarter.

 

Net sales for the Company’s Other segment, which primarily includes certain products of American Fruits and Flavors, LLC, a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, sold to independent third-party customers, decreased 29.5 percent to $4.8 million for the 2026 third quarter, from $6.8 million in the 2025 third quarter.

 

Net sales to customers outside the United States increased 28.1 percent to $1.20 billion in the 2026 third quarter, from $937.1 million in the 2025 third quarter, representing approximately 47 percent and 43 percent of total reported net sales for the 2026 and 2025 third quarters, respectively. Net sales to customers outside the United States, on a foreign currency adjusted basis (non-GAAP), increased 27.2 percent to $1.19 billion in the 2026 third quarter.

 

Gross profit as a percentage of net sales for the 2026 third quarter was 54.7 percent, compared with 55.7 percent in the 2025 third quarter. The decrease in gross profit as a percentage of net sales for the 2026 third quarter was primarily the result of increased aluminum can costs, geographical sales mix and increased freight-in costs, partially offset by pricing actions. Adjusted gross profit (non-GAAP) as a percentage of net sales, excluding the Alcohol Brands segment, for the 2026 third quarter was 54.9 percent, compared with 56.2 percent in the 2025 third quarter.

 

Distribution expenses for the 2026 third quarter were $116.3 million, or 4.5 percent of net sales, compared with $82.6 million, or 3.8 percent of net sales, in the 2025 third quarter. The increase in distribution expenses for the 2026 third quarter was primarily due to increased freight and fuel expenses.

 

Selling expenses for the 2026 third quarter were $260.1 million, or 10.1 percent of net sales, compared with $214.6 million, or 9.8 percent of net sales, in the 2025 third quarter. The increase in selling expenses for the 2026 third quarter was primarily due to increased social, digital, media and other marketing expenses, including sponsorships and endorsements, in order to reach a broader consumer audience and increase household penetration.

 

General and administrative expenses for the 2026 third quarter were $305.7 million, or 11.9 percent of net sales, compared with $251.9 million, or 11.5 percent of net sales, for the 2025 third quarter. Stock-based compensation was $30.4 million for the 2026 third quarter, compared with $32.8 million in the 2025 third quarter. General and administrative expenses for the 2026 third quarter included a property and equipment impairment charge of $19.9 million and expenses of $14.7 million related to the Company’s digital transformation initiatives. Adjusted general and administrative expenses (non-GAAP) for the 2026 third quarter were $274.4 million, compared with $230.9 million in the 2025 third quarter. Adjusted general and administrative expenses (non-GAAP) as a percentage of net sales, less the Alcohol Brands segment, were 10.8 percent and 10.7 percent for the 2026 and 2025 third quarters, respectively.

Operating expenses for the 2026 third quarter were $682.0 million, compared with $549.1 million in the 2025 third quarter. Operating expenses for the 2026 third quarter included a property and equipment impairment charge of $19.9 million and expenses of $14.7 million related to the Company’s digital transformation initiatives. Adjusted operating expenses (non-GAAP) for the 2026 third quarter were $644.4 million, compared with $517.8 million in the 2025 third quarter. Operating expenses as a percentage of net sales for the 2026 third quarter were 26.6 percent, compared with 25.0 percent in the 2025 third quarter. Adjusted operating expenses (non-GAAP) as a percentage of net sales, less the Alcohol Brands segment, were 25.4 percent and 23.9 percent for the 2026 and 2025 third quarters, respectively.

 

Operating income for the 2026 third quarter increased 6.4 percent to $718.7 million, from $675.4 million in the 2025 third quarter. Adjusted operating income (non-GAAP) for the 2026 third quarter increased 7.1 percent to $748.1 million, from $698.4 million in the 2025 third quarter.

 

The effective tax rate for the 2026 third quarter was 24.6 percent, compared with 23.9 percent in the 2025 third quarter. The increase in the effective tax rate was primarily due to a lower stock-based compensation deduction and an increase in income from higher income tax jurisdictions, partially offset by a lower valuation allowance on foreign tax credit carryforwards.

 

Net income for the 2026 third quarter increased 8.4 percent to $568.4 million, from $524.5 million in the 2025 third quarter. Adjusted net income (non-GAAP) for the 2026 third quarter increased 9.0 percent to $591.1 million, from $542.1 million in the 2025 third quarter. Net income per diluted share for the 2026 third quarter increased 7.8 percent to $0.29, from $0.27 in the 2025 third quarter. Adjusted net income per diluted share (non-GAAP) for the 2026 third quarter increased 8.5 percent to $0.30, from $0.28 in the 2025 third quarter.

 

Hilton H. Schlosberg, Chief Executive Officer, said, “We delivered another quarter of strong top-line performance in both our domestic and international markets, with record quarterly net sales increasing 16.7 percent to $2.56 billion.

 

“Our net sales to customers outside the United States increased 28.1 percent in the 2026 third quarter to approximately 47 percent of total net sales. LATAM, in particular, had a solid 2026 third quarter with net sales increasing 48.8 percent in dollars, primarily driven by our operations in Brazil.

 

“During the 2026 third quarter, we continued our increased marketing investments across a variety of new platforms and partnerships, including social, digital and media, to support both existing product offerings and new product launches, as well as reach a broadening consumer base for the Company.

 

“Our new product launches this quarter, including additions to the Monster Energy®, Java Monster®, Mother® and FLRT™ product lines, reflect our ongoing commitment to innovation alongside the continued growth of our core offerings,” Mr. Schlosberg added.

 

2026 Nine-Months Results

 

Net sales for the nine-months ended September 30, 2026 increased 20.9 percent to $7.45 billion, from $6.16 billion in the comparable period last year. Net changes in foreign currency exchange rates had a favorable impact of $145.9 million on net sales for the nine-months ended September 30, 2026. Net sales on a foreign currency adjusted basis (non-GAAP) increased 18.6 percent in the nine-months ended September 30, 2026. Net sales, excluding the Alcohol Brands segment, on a foreign currency adjusted basis (non-GAAP), increased 19.1 percent in the nine-months ended September 30, 2026.

 

Gross profit as a percentage of net sales for the nine-months ended September 30, 2026 was 55.2 percent, compared with 56.0 percent in the comparable period last year.

Share Repurchase Program

 

During the 2026 third quarter, the Company purchased approximately 2.3 million shares of its common stock at an average purchase price of $43.87 per share, for a total amount of approximately $100.0 million. As of November 4, 2026, approximately $800.0 million remained available for repurchase under the previously authorized repurchase programs.

 

Stock Split

 

As previously announced, the Company’s Board of Directors approved and declared a two-for-one stock split of its common stock, effected in the form of a 100 percent stock dividend. The stock dividend was issued on August 10, 2026, and the Company’s common stock began trading at the split-adjusted price on August 11, 2026.
