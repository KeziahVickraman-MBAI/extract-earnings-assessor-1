Aethlon Medical Reports Second Quarter Fiscal 2027 Results and Provides Corporate Update

 

SAN DIEGO, November 12, 2026 -- Aethlon Medical, Inc. (the Company or Aethlon) (Nasdaq: AEMD), a clinical-stage medical therapeutic company, today announced financial results for fiscal second quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a corporate update.

 

Corporate Updates

 

Merger

 

On September 17, 2026, we entered into a Plan of Merger and Reorganization (the “Merger Agreement”) with North Immunology, Inc. (“North Immunology”) and two wholly owned subsidiaries of the Company, pursuant to which the parties agreed to effect a two-step merger transaction (the “Merger”). Upon completion of the proposed Merger, the combined company is expected to operate under the name North Immunology, Inc.

 

In connection with the proposed Merger we implemented workforce reductions near the end of the second fiscal quarter. We have retained key personnel necessary to maintain our corporate and regulatory obligations and continue management of our Australian oncology clinical trial. We remain operational and continue to evaluate our activities and resource requirements in light of the proposed Merger and our available financial resources. The Merger Agreement contemplates the wind-down of certain activities associated with our legacy business upon and subject to the closing of the transaction. The timing and extent of any further changes to our operations will depend on the progress and outcome of the proposed Merger, our available resources and other factors. There can be no assurance that the Merger will be completed on the anticipated terms or within the anticipated timeframe, or at all.

 

In connection with the Merger, holders of Aethlon’s common stock are expected to receive a contingent value right (“CVR”) pursuant to a contingent value rights agreement to be entered into at closing. The CVRs are intended to provide shareholders with the opportunity to participate in future potential proceeds, if any, resulting from the monetization of Aethlon’s legacy Hemopurifier business. Any payments under the CVR will be subject to the terms and conditions of the CVR agreement. There can be no assurance that any monetization transaction will occur or that CVR holders will receive any payments.

 

Clinical Highlights

 

Phase 1 oncology study: “We advanced our clinical program during the quarter,” said James Frakes, CEO and CFO. “We treated the second participant in the third and final dosing cohort of our Phase 1 oncology study in Australia. We have treated eight patients across the three dosing cohorts to date and continue to pursue enrollment of one additional patient. Given the anticipated timing of the proposed Merger with North Immunology, we have a limited window in which to complete enrollment. We intend to conclude patient recruitment in connection with the proposed Merger and subsequently compile the available clinical data for analysis.

 

Financial Highlights

 

·Cash and liquidity: Cash and cash equivalents were approximately $6.5 million as of September 30, 2026.
·Operating expenses: Consolidated operating expenses for quarter increased 77.2% to approximately $2.7 million versus approximately $1.5 million in the prior-year quarter. This increase was primarily attributable to higher professional fees associated with the proposed Merger and the lack of recognition of a research and development tax credit in the current period due to timing, which resulted in higher general and administrative expenses in the current-year period compared to the prior-year period.

 

The consolidated balance sheets for September 30, 2026, and March 31, 2026 and the consolidated statements of operations for the fiscal quarters and six-month periods ended September 30, 2026, and 2025, are included at the end of this release.

About the Hemopurifier®

 

The Aethlon Hemopurifier is an investigational medical device designed to remove enveloped viruses and tumor-derived extracellular vesicles (EVs) from circulation. It is used extracorporeally with a blood pump and combines plasma separation, size exclusion, and affinity binding using a plant lectin resin that targets mannose-rich surfaces found on EVs and viruses. EVs released by solid tumors are believed to play a role in metastasis and the resistance to immunotherapies and chemotherapy. Removal of enveloped viruses and extracellular vesicles has been demonstrated in both in vitro studies and human subjects.

 

The Hemopurifier holds a U.S. Food and Drug Administration Breakthrough Device Designation for:

 

The treatment of individuals with advanced or metastatic cancer unresponsive to or intolerant of standard-of-care therapy; and the treatment of life-threatening viruses not addressed with approved therapies.

 

About Aethlon Medical, Inc.

 

Aethlon Medical, Inc. (Nasdaq: AEMD) is a clinical-stage medical therapeutic company headquartered in San Diego, California. The Company is advancing the Hemopurifier®, an investigational extracorporeal immunotherapeutic platform designed to remove tumor-derived extracellular vesicles and enveloped viruses from circulation for potential applications in oncology, infectious disease, and other disease states.
