ALLIANCE RESOURCE PARTNERS, L.P.

​

Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial and Operating Results; Declares Quarterly Cash Distribution of $0.60 Per Unit; and Updates Guidance

​

2026 Quarter Highlights

​

●Total revenues of $603.0 million and net income attributable to ARLP of $139.6 million, up year-over-year 5.5% and 46.8%, respectively
●Adjusted EBITDA attributable to ARLP of $233.9 million, up 26.3% year-over-year
●Segment Adjusted EBITDA Expense per ton sold at our Coal Operations improved 13.1% year-over-year and 7.9% sequentially
●Record oil & gas royalty revenues and volumes, driven by $223.5 million of acquisitions completed during the quarter
●Distributable Cash Flow of $136.8 million and Distribution Coverage Ratio of 1.76x improved over 27% sequentially
●Declares quarterly cash distribution of $0.60 per unit, or $2.40 per unit annualized

​

TULSA, OKLAHOMA, October 26, 2026 — Alliance Resource Partners, L.P. (NASDAQ: ARLP) (“we,” “us,” “our,” “ARLP” or the “Partnership”) today reported financial and operating results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026 (the “2026 Quarter” and “2026 Period,” respectively). This release includes comparisons of results to the three and nine months ended September 30, 2025 (the “2025 Quarter” and “2025 Period,” respectively) and to the quarter ended June 30, 2026 (the “Sequential Quarter”).

The use of “attributable to ARLP” indicates the exclusion of noncontrolling interests. For a definition of EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA attributable to ARLP and Segment Adjusted EBITDA Expense and related reconciliations to comparable GAAP financial measures, please see the end of this release.

●Total revenues in the 2026 Quarter increased 5.5% and 9.3% compared to the 2025 Quarter and Sequential Quarter, respectively, as a result of record oil & gas royalty revenues, increased coal sales volumes and higher other revenues, partially offset by lower coal sales price per ton.
●Net income attributable to ARLP increased 46.8% and 75.5% compared to the 2025 Quarter and Sequential Quarter, respectively, primarily as a result of higher total revenues and equity method investment income, lower operating expenses, a $16.5 million increase in the fair value of our digital assets and a $6.3 million gain recognized as a result of the remeasurement of our previously held equity investment in AllDale III in connection with the AllDale III & IV acquisition. Partially offsetting these increases were higher income tax expenses and a $7.3 million loss on the derivative instruments acquired in the AllDale III & IV acquisition.
●Adjusted EBITDA attributable to ARLP increased 26.3% and 26.4% compared to the 2025 Quarter and Sequential Quarter, respectively, primarily driven by higher Coal Operations Segment Adjusted EBITDA, the contribution from the AllDale III & IV acquisition and higher equity method investment income, partially offset by higher general and administrative expenses.

​

CEO Commentary

“We had an outstanding third quarter led by our Coal Operations’ strong shipping quarter combined with Hamilton being back on longwall production and River View and Tunnel Ridge operating well, which drove Segment Adjusted EBITDA at our Coal Operations 23.1% higher sequentially to $186.8 million, while Segment Adjusted EBITDA Expense per ton sold declined 13.1% year-over-year,” said Joseph W. Craft III, Chairman, President and Chief Executive Officer. “The capital we have invested in our operations over the past several years is translating into a lower-cost, more reliable platform.”

Mr. Craft added, “In our first full quarter including AllDale III & IV, our Oil & Gas Royalties segment generated Segment Adjusted EBITDA attributable to ARLP of $43.6 million, up 16.8% sequentially and 61.4% year-over-year. The acquisition meaningfully increases the segment’s contribution to ARLP’s consolidated cash flows and expands our exposure to natural gas, which we believe will continue to benefit from growing LNG exports and rising power demand.”

Balance Sheet and Liquidity

As of September 30, 2026, total debt and finance leases were outstanding in the amount of $679.9 million. The Partnership’s total and net leverage ratios were 0.89 times and 0.78 times debt to trailing twelve months Adjusted EBITDA attributable to ARLP, respectively, as of September 30, 2026. ARLP ended the 2026 Quarter with total liquidity of $430.8 million, which included $84.8 million of cash and cash equivalents and $346.0 million of borrowings available under its revolving credit and accounts receivable securitization facilities. In addition, ARLP held 673 bitcoins valued at $56.3 million as of September 30, 2026.

Distributions

ARLP announced today that the Board of Directors of ARLP’s general partner approved a cash distribution to unitholders for the 2026 Quarter of $0.60 per unit (an annualized rate of $2.40 per unit), payable on November 13, 2026, to all unitholders of record as of the close of trading on November 6, 2026. The Distribution Coverage Ratio for the 2026 Quarter was 1.76x.

Concurrent with this announcement, we are providing qualified notice to brokers and nominees that hold ARLP units on behalf of non-U.S. investors under Treasury Regulation Section 1.1446-4(b) and (d) and Treasury Regulation Section 1.1446(f)-4(c)(2)(iii). Brokers and nominees should treat one hundred percent (100%) of ARLP’s distributions to non-U.S. investors as being attributable to income that is effectively connected with a United States trade or business. In addition, brokers and nominees should treat one hundred percent (100%) of the distribution as being in excess of cumulative net income for purposes of determining the amount to withhold. Accordingly, ARLP’s distributions to non-U.S. investors are subject to federal income tax withholding at a rate equal to the highest applicable effective tax rate plus ten percent (10%). Nominees, and not ARLP, are treated as the withholding agents responsible for withholding on the distributions received by them on behalf of non-U.S. investors.

Outlook

“We believe the fundamentals supporting coal demand continue to build,” Mr. Craft continued. “Electricity load in the markets we serve is growing while permitting, interconnection and supply chain constraints are slowing the pace at which new generation can come online. We believe this dynamic will extend the runway for the existing coal fleet, with the impact becoming increasingly visible in our customers’ coal burn as data center-driven demand materializes.”

“We continue to convert this constructive market into contracted volumes. During the quarter, we added 4.2 million committed and priced tons through 2028, and we are essentially fully committed at the midpoint of our 2026 sales volume guidance. With 31.7 million tons committed and priced for 2027, we enter next year with substantial visibility and the operating capacity to capture additional demand as commercial opportunities develop.”

Mr. Craft added, “Export markets have become a more meaningful component of our sales portfolio, with 3.7 million tons committed and priced for 2026 and 3.0 million tons for 2027. Market conditions remain dynamic, and we remain cautiously optimistic that additional export opportunities could provide upside to our sales volumes next year.”

Mr. Craft concluded, “In our Oil & Gas Royalties segment, if today’s crude oil prices remain elevated, we could be in a position to have another record quarter to finish the year. Reconciliation of Non-GAAP Financial Measures (Unaudited)

Reconciliation of GAAP “Net income” to non-GAAP “EBITDA,” “Adjusted EBITDA,” “Adjusted EBITDA attributable to ARLP,” “Distribution Coverage Ratio” and “Distributable Cash Flow” (in thousands).

EBITDA is defined as net income before net interest expense, income taxes and depreciation, depletion and amortization, Adjusted EBITDA is EBITDA adjusted for certain items that we characterize as unrepresentative of our ongoing operations and Adjusted EBITDA attributable to ARLP is Adjusted EBITDA excluding amounts attributable to noncontrolling interests. Distributable cash flow (“DCF”) is defined as Adjusted EBITDA attributable to ARLP excluding equity method investment earnings, interest expense (before capitalized interest), interest income, income taxes and estimated maintenance capital expenditures and adding distributions from equity method investments.  Distribution coverage ratio (“DCR”) is defined as DCF divided by distributions paid to partners.  

Management believes that the presentation of such additional financial measures provides useful information to investors regarding our performance and results of operations because these measures, when used in conjunction with related GAAP financial measures, (i) provide additional information about our core operating performance and ability to generate and distribute cash flow, (ii) provide investors with the financial analytical framework upon which management bases financial, operational, compensation and planning decisions and (iii) present measurements that investors, rating agencies and debt holders have indicated are useful in assessing us and our results of operations.

EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA attributable to ARLP, DCF and DCR should not be considered as alternatives to income from operations, net income, net income attributable to ARLP, cash flows from operating activities or any other measure of financial performance presented in accordance with GAAP.  EBITDA and DCF are not intended to represent cash flow and do not represent the measure of cash available for distribution.  Our method of computing EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA attributable to ARLP, DCF and DCR may not be the same method used to compute similar measures reported by other companies, or EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA attributable to ARLP, DCF and DCR may be computed differently by us in different contexts (i.e., public reporting versus computation under financing agreements).
